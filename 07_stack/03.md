# 03.asm

`RAX = 10, RSP = 0x7fffffffdbb0`

`call calculate`:It pushes the address of `after_call` (0x40100a) onto the stack.
RSP drops by 8.

Inside `calculate`:
`add rax, 5`: `RAX` becomes 15 (0xf). The stack is not modified.

`ret`:does a pop into the program counter (RIP). It reads 0x40100a off the top of the stack and jumps execution back to `after_call`. `RSP` increases by 8 to 0x7fffffffdbb0.

The stack's stores info to tell the processor how to get back.

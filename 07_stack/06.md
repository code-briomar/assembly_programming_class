# 06.md

ESP = 0xffffcdd0 at the start

`push eax`:
ESP drops by 4 bytes: 0xffffcdd0 to
0xffffcdcc.
0x11223344 is placed in ESP.

`push ebx`:
ESP drops by 4 bytes: 0xffffcdcc to
0xffffcdc8.
0x00001234 is placed in EBX.

`pop ebx`:
Top value (0x1234) goes back into EBX.
ESP rises by 4: 0xffffcdc8 to
0xffffcdcc.

`pop eax`:
Next value (0x11223344) goes back into EAX.
ESP rises by 4: 0xffffcdcc to
0xffffcdd0.

Saving 32 bit registers in matching LIFO order

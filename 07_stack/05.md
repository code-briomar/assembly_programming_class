# 05.asm

RSP = 0x7fffffffdbb0 at the start.

`push rax`: 0x1122334455667788 to RAX
RSP drops by 8: 0x7fffffffdbb0 to 0x7fffffffdba8.

`push rbx`: 0x0000000000001234 to RBX
RSP drops by 8: 0x7fffffffdba8 to 0x7fffffffdba0.

`pop rbx`: Reads top of stack (0x1234) back into RBX.
RSP goes up by 8: 0x7fffffffdba0 to 0x7fffffffdba8.

`pop rax`:
Reads next value (0x1122334455667788) back into RAX.RSP goes up by 8: 0x7fffffffdba8 to
0x7fffffffdbb0.

Both registers ( RAX and rbx ) keep their original values since the order of popping is reverse to the order of pushing.
